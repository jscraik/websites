Intellicheck

We helped Intellicheck tell their story of being the best experience customers never knew they had.

Project overview

About the client

Intellicheck is an ID validation company. In 2023, Intellicheck validated over 100M identities, all in seconds.

What we did

Whiteboard partnered with Intellicheck on a fresh identity system and website. I really enjoy projects where I get to collaborate with our very talented identity designers. The brand guides we receive are full of potential for great experiences.

Early on, the identity team pinpointed that customer service was one of Intellicheck's core values. The tagline: "The best customer experience your customer never knew they had" became a rallying cry within their organization. And the invisible layer—depicted as a layer of frosted glass—became one of the central visual themes of the website.
