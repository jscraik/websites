About

Welcome. I'm Jose, a brand designer and Webflow developer. I started my career in design over 14 years ago, learning on the job at a small real estate firm.

In 2018 I started my own independent studio. For two years I served clients and ran a small business.

In 2020, three months before the pandemic, I joined Whiteboard as a part of the web studio where I designed and developed over a hundred websites and grew to become a creative director.

In 2024 I became an in-house brand designer, first at Aspect and now at Webflow.

I'm so grateful to be working in this field, doing something that brings me so much joy. Thanks for visiting.

Skills

Website and Visual Design

I’m a skilled, award-winning visual designer. In my career I’ve likely designed more than 150 websites (I’ve lost count!). The foundational principles I use in website design I carry into multiple deliverable types like deck templates, emails, social ads, video graphics, print design and more.

Branding

I started my career with logo design. I’ve designed hundreds. Brands are much more than logos of course. And I love extending a brand’s visual system with all the visual tools it needs to be flexible.

Illustration

I use strong illustration skills to support my overall design work. I can design icon libraries, spot illustrations, and patterns. I’m learning to illustrate people, but I’m still quite limited at that.

Webflow Development

I’m an expert Webflow developer. I consider performance, accessibility, animation, responsiveness, and ease-of-use in each build.
